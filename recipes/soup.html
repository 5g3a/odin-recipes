Kielbasa Pierogi Soup

Home

Description

Pillowy pierogi, hearty kielbasa, and tender cabbage bulk up paprika-flavored broth for a tasty, yet quick soup. For extra flavor, brown the pierogi in batches with 2 tablespoons butter in an extra-large skillet before adding them to the soup.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 (14-ounce) package Polish kielbasa, cut into half moons
  • 1/2 medium head green cabbage, chopped (about 5 cups)
  • 1 cup chopped yellow onion
  • 1 large carrot, peeled and chopped
  • 1 large stalk celery, chopped, plus leaves for garnish
  • 8 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon caraway seeds
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 (24- to 26-ounce) package frozen mini pierogi (such as Mrs. T’s)
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice or apple cider vinegar
  • 1/3 cup fresh dill, stemmed, plus more for garnish
  • sour cream, for serving

Steps

  1. Heat oil over medium heat in a 6- to 8-quart Dutch oven. Add kielbasa; cook, stirring occasionally, until browned, about 5 minutes. Use a slotted spoon to transfer kielbasa to a paper towel-lined plate.
  2. Increase heat to medium-high. Add cabbage; cook, stirring occasionally, until browned, 4 to 5 minutes. Reduce heat to medium. Add onion, carrot, and celery; cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, 3 to 4 minutes.
  3. Return kielbasa to the pot; stir in broth, mustard, paprika, caraway seeds, and salt. Add pierogi to soup. Cook, uncovered, until pierogi are tender and float, about 5 minutes. Stir in lemon juice and dill. Garnish with celery leaves and additional dill. Serve with sour cream.
